## Scope and legal note

This audit is structural analysis, not legal advice. Jurisdictions in scope: [list].
Employment and equality law varies between them, and anything material here — assessment
design, adjustments, automated screening, record retention, and any change to how decisions
are recorded — should be reviewed by local employment counsel before implementation. Where
the audit flags a risk of a stage disadvantaging a group, that is a structural finding
pointing at where to look; it is not an adverse-impact determination, and any analysis of
outcomes by demographic group is normally run through counsel.
