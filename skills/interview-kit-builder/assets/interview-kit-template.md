## 7. Interviewer instructions

**Opening (2 minutes, every stage):** Who you are and what you do. What this stage assesses
and how long it runs. That you will leave time for their questions. When they will hear back.

**During:**
- Ask the primary questions as written. Vary the probes, not the primaries — comparison needs
  a common baseline.
- Go three layers deep on one example rather than one layer on three.
- Use silence. The three seconds after an answer ends are often the most useful part.
- Write what was said, not how it felt. Specifics, decisions, numbers, short quotes.
- Note what was missing. An absent answer is evidence.

**After:**
- Score before your next meeting, while the evidence is fresh.
- **Submit the scorecard before the debrief.** Not optional. Independent scores are the entire
  reason for running multiple interviewers.

**Do not ask about:** family or family plans, age or graduation year, health or disability,
nationality or birthplace, religion, marital status, or anything else you would not ask every
candidate for this role. If there is a real underlying concern, the compliant version is in
the kit's guardrails section.

## 8. Debrief

- **When:** [within X hours of the final stage]
- **Attending:** [names]
- **Entry condition:** all scorecards submitted. No scorecard, no seat.
- **Order:** most junior interviewer first, hiring manager last.
- **Format:** competency by competency, evidence before conclusions. Discuss the splits — they
  are the most useful thing in the room.
- **Decision-maker:** [name]. The others' scores are input, not votes.
- **Output:** decision, the two-line reason, and — if hired — the development note that
  transfers to onboarding.
