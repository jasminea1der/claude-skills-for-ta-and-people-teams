## 8. Team-level action

Most of what drives engagement is local — the manager relationship, workload and its
predictability, clarity about the job, whether contribution is recognised. Almost none of it
is fixed centrally, and central programmes get built to address it because the centre is who
reads the results.

**Split:**

| Owned centrally | Owned by managers |
|---|---|
| [structural items: pay architecture, career framework, tooling, a broken process, a policy] | [local items: workload, clarity, recognition, team communication, development conversations] |

**Evidence for the split:** items with high variance between teams are local; items with low
variance are structural. [State what the variance showed.]

**What every manager commits to:** one or two team-level actions, recorded by [date], visible
to their team.

**How the centre supports it:** [the pack, the conversation structure, the People partner
time, the deadline, and what happens for managers whose scores are poor].
