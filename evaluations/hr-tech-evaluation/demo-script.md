# HR tech evaluation: controlled demo and reference guide

Use the same scenarios, order, time budget, attendees, and scoring anchors for every
vendor. Send the scenarios 7–14 days in advance and require a configured environment.
Vendors must show the product live; a claim or slide is not a demonstration.

## 90-minute demo format

| Block | Time | Owner |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Framing and rules | 5 min | Buyer |
| Vendor overview | 10 min | Vendor |
| Scenarios 1–4 | 50 min | Vendor / buyer |
| Buyer drives one task | 10 min | Buyer |
| Unanticipated report | 5 min | Buyer |
| Open questions and follow-ups | 10 min | Buyer |

Score independently within one hour. Discuss only after every evaluator has submitted a
score and one line of evidence per requirement.

## Scenarios

### 1. Volume day and approval exception

Create a requisition for a new role with two approvers, then show what happens when the
second approver is on leave. Move three candidates through the workflow and show the
notifications, audit trail, and recruiter workload. The team must be able to identify what
is configurable without vendor services.

**Probe:** Who changes the approval chain, how long does it take, and what is recorded?

### 2. Correction and bulk action

Reject forty candidates, accidentally reject one candidate who should remain in process, then
correct the mistake. Show the audit trail, candidate communication, and whether a bulk action
can be safely reversed. This is especially important when migrating off SuccessFactors
Recruiting and ensuring staff can handle higher-volume exception work without custom scripts
or manual rework.

**Probe:** What can an admin undo, what requires support, and what cannot be undone?

### 3. Untrained hiring manager and candidate journey

Hand the keyboard to a hiring manager who has not seen the product. They must review a
shortlist and submit independent feedback from a phone or mobile-sized view. Separately,
time a candidate applying from a phone, uploading a document, scheduling an interview, and
checking status.

**Probe:** Can interviewers see others’ feedback before submitting? What happens when a
candidate cancels or reapplies?

### 4. Handoff, permissions, and reporting

Show a confidential requisition and prove who cannot see it. Complete an offer acceptance
and show the fields sent to the HRIS / EC. Then build a stage-conversion report by
department and month for the last two quarters, including a custom field not mentioned in
advance.

**Probe:** Is the integration native, partner-built, or API-based? Show the API
documentation, error handling, retry path, and export of the same data.

## Questions to ask every vendor

1. What can we configure ourselves, and what requires professional services?
2. Show the audit trail for the last change.
3. Show this data exported, then show the API documentation.
4. What is the most common support ticket for this workflow?
5. Which customers at our scale and in our jurisdictions can we speak to?
6. What did the last implementation of this size get wrong?
7. What happens when the product is wrong or unavailable during a critical hiring event?
8. For each AI feature: what does it do, does it influence an employment decision, what data
   trains it, can we opt out, what bias testing exists, and where is human oversight?

## Reference calls

Ask for three references where possible: one at similar scale and jurisdictions, one
implemented in the last 12 months, and one that migrated from the incumbent or a comparable
system. Add at least one independently sourced reference if practical.

Ask:

- What took longer than expected?
- What would you do differently?
- What does your team complain about?
- Who administers the system day to day, and how much time does it take?
- What did you have to change to fit the tool?
- What did you assume it would do that it does not do?
- What happened the last time a critical workflow broke?
- How accurate was migration, including documents and historic records?
- Would you buy it again? What would have to be true for you to switch?
